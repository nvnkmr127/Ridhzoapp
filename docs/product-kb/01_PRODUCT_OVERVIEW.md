# Ridhzo — Product Overview

## One-line summary
**Ridhzo is a simple, mobile-first lead CRM that captures every lead automatically, alerts you instantly, and lets you reply on WhatsApp in one tap — so no lead goes cold.**

Official short description: *"Lead capture, instant alerts, and one-tap messaging."*

## The problem Ridhzo solves
Small businesses and sales teams spend money on Facebook ads, Google ads, websites and referrals — then lose most of those leads because:

- Leads sit in Facebook Lead Center, Google sheets, email inboxes and WhatsApp chats — **scattered everywhere**.
- Nobody calls back fast enough. A lead contacted in the first 5 minutes is far more likely to convert than one contacted after an hour.
- Follow-ups are forgotten. Most sales need 5+ touches; most reps stop after 1 or 2.
- Owners have no idea which ad, which source or which salesperson is actually bringing money.
- Big CRMs (Salesforce, Zoho, HubSpot) are too complex, too expensive and not built for the phone.

## The Ridhzo solution — the lead journey
Ridhzo follows one lead from the first click to the closed deal:

1. **Capture** — Leads flow in automatically from Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads, Google Lead Form Ads, website forms, your own hosted forms, any tool via webhook/API, CSV import, missed calls, or quick manual entry.
2. **Clean** — Duplicates are detected by phone/email and can be merged automatically.
3. **Route** — The lead is assigned to the right person instantly (round-robin, capacity-based, team rules, or automations).
4. **Alert** — The owner gets a push notification on their phone, an in-app alert with sound, email, or WhatsApp alert within seconds.
5. **Respond** — One tap opens WhatsApp, a call or email with a ready-made, personalised template. AI can draft the reply.
6. **Follow up** — Reminders, automatic drip sequences, meetings and site visits make sure no lead is forgotten.
7. **Automate** — WHEN → IF → THEN automations do the repetitive work (assign, change status, send WhatsApp, schedule follow-up, enroll in sequence).
8. **Convert & measure** — Pipeline board, win/loss, dashboards and insights show what is working — by source, by rep, by stage.

## Key differentiators (vs. generic CRMs)
1. **Lead-first, not contact-database-first.** Every screen is about moving a lead forward today.
2. **WhatsApp-native.** One-tap personal WhatsApp for free, or official WhatsApp Business API for automation, templates, campaigns and sequences.
3. **Speed-to-lead built-in.** Auto-assignment + instant push + one-tap reply.
4. **Real AI help, not gimmicks.** AI Assistant that can search leads, update status, set reminders and draft messages (you approve before anything is sent).
5. **Field-sales ready.** Meetings, site visits, GPS check-in, public booking page.
6. **Priced for small businesses.** ₹249/month for Starter; unlimited users and leads for ₹449/month.
7. **Multi-language app.** English, Hindi, Telugu.

## Technology & reliability (plain language)
- Cloud-hosted web app, works in any modern browser; installable on Android & iPhone home screen.
- Each business's data is fully isolated from others (multi-tenant isolation on every query).
- Background job system retries failed deliveries automatically (webhooks, reminders, messages).
- Passwords are hashed; email passwords and API secrets are encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
- Complete audit log of who changed what.
