# Lead Profile

## What it is
Every lead has one page that holds **everything** about them — contact details, what they asked for, every conversation, tasks, meetings, files and next steps. Open it and you know the full story in 5 seconds.

## What's on the lead profile
| Section | What you see / do |
|---|---|
| **Header** | Name, phone, email, company, status, owner, priority, score, source |
| **One-tap actions** | Call, WhatsApp, SMS, Email — pre-filled with your templates |
| **Status & owner controls** | Change status or reassign in one tap (history is recorded) |
| **"What they told you in the form"** | All answers from the ad or web form |
| **Custom fields** | Your business-specific fields, editable inline |
| **Tags** | Add/remove tags |
| **Activity timeline** | Every note, call, WhatsApp, email, status change, assignment, meeting and system event in time order |
| **WhatsApp conversation** | Threaded chat view with delivery/read status (Business API mode) |
| **Follow-ups** | Upcoming and past reminders; add a new one |
| **Meetings** | Scheduled meetings/site visits, outcomes |
| **Sequences** | Which drip sequences the lead is in; add to a sequence in one tap |
| **Attachments** | Upload and view files (quotes, ID proofs, brochures) |
| **Shared content** | Links/brochures you shared and whether the lead opened them |
| **AI summary** | One-click AI brief of who the lead is and where things stand |
| **AI reply draft** | Draft the next WhatsApp/email in a chosen tone and language |
| **Next best action** | Suggested next step (e.g., "Send template", "Reschedule follow-up", "Close the deal", "Qualify lead") |
| **Best time to contact** | When this lead is most likely to respond |
| **Re-engagement plan** | For cold leads — a 4-step win-back schedule |
| **Audit export** | Full chronological history export of the lead |

## Real use cases
- **Handover:** A rep goes on leave; the new owner reads the timeline and AI summary and continues the conversation without asking the customer to repeat themselves.
- **Call prep:** Before calling, a rep sees the lead opened the pricing brochure twice yesterday — and leads with pricing.
- **Proof & documents:** A loan agent keeps the customer's documents as attachments on the lead.

## Why it matters
- No more "who spoke to this customer last and what did they say?"
- Consistent, informed conversations → higher conversion.
- Everything is logged automatically — reps don't have to write reports.
