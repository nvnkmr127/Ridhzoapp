# Follow-ups & Reminders

## What it is
Most sales happen after the 5th follow-up — and most people stop after the 1st. Ridhzo makes sure every lead has a next step and nobody forgets it.

## Key capabilities
- **Create a follow-up** on any lead: title, date & time, notes, type (call, WhatsApp, meeting, task).
- **Reminders** delivered on time by push notification, in-app bell and (optionally) email.
- **Follow-ups page** — list of Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Completed.
- **Calendar view** — see follow-ups by day/week/month.
- **Complete** with one tap; the lead's "last contacted" and "next follow-up" update automatically.
- **Overdue escalation** — overdue follow-ups are ranked by severity (24h+ = High, 48h+ = Critical) so managers can step in.
- **Automatic follow-ups** — automations can schedule follow-ups (e.g., "new lead → follow up in 1 day").
- **Follow-up when closing a meeting** — record a meeting outcome and schedule the next follow-up in the same step.
- **Business hours & timezone aware** — reminders respect your workspace timezone.
- **Mobile API** — follow-ups are available to the Ridhzo mobile app.

## Real use cases
- **Callback promised:** Lead says "call me Saturday 11 AM" → rep sets a follow-up → Saturday 11 AM phone buzzes with the lead's name and a one-tap call button.
- **Nurture after a site visit:** Follow-up set for 2 days after every site visit automatically.
- **Manager oversight:** Monday morning the manager sees 12 critical overdue follow-ups for one rep and redistributes them.

## Why it matters
- Converts more leads with zero extra ad spend.
- Reps start every day with a clear to-do list.
- Managers see exactly what is falling through the cracks.
